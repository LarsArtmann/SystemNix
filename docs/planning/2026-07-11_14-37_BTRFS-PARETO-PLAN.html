01Summary

The Problem SystemNix's BTRFS setup is production-hardened and goes far beyond wiki guidance in monitoring and safety. However, two structural gaps (no remote backup, no subvolume separation for /nix and /home) and several monitoring gaps (no scrub alerting, no compression ratio tracking) leave data at risk.
The Plan 25 tasks, each 12 minutes or less, sorted by Pareto impact. Tier 1 (critical: prevent data loss) delivers 51% of the value in 50 minutes. Tiers 1+2 together deliver 64% in 106 minutes. All 25 tasks deliver 100% in ~4 hours.
