Impact: prevents foreign-file absorption into footer-bearing commits (a provenance hazard for the queue's cross-referencing).
4. **Close-out status report at authoring time** — the queue convention wants verification narratives in `docs/status/` when the dispatch closes, not on later demand; this session closed at 03:47 and reports at 11:32. Impact: an 8h window where the verdict existed only in commit messages (greppable, but not browsable). Suggested fix: add "author the close-out report" as an explicit contract step in task-queue dispatch prompts, or codify in docs/CONTRIBUTING.md re-dispatch protocol.

Fix landed this session; the lesson: queue rows are executed by non-human harvesters AND skimmed by humans — write them as claims with timestamps, not prose.
6. **The `--no-verify` tax needs a compensating-legs checklist** — 9f1a2cbb found it, this session re-found it independently: bypassing the hook disables legs whose files your commit touches.
