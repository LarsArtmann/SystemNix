## d) TOTALLY FUCKED UP

Nothing rose to that level. Two honest design caveats shipped UNDOCUMENTED
(should have been in the CHANGELOG entry):

1. **The global timeout ABANDONS, it does not CANCEL.** `tokio::time::
   timeout` around `spawn_blocking` returns control to the daemon at the
   budget, but the blocking thread keeps walking until the OS-level walk
   finishes (tokio cannot cancel a blocking task). So the worst-case IO
   footprint of one pathological root is bounded by per-root budgets —
   NOT by the global timeout. The owner constraint ("no infinite loops,
   no IO pressure") is still met, but the mental model "global timeout
   stops the walk" is wrong and the CHANGELOG says nothing about it.
2. **`measure_dir` (the unbounded walker) remains a public API** now that
   no production caller uses it — a footgun for the exact tree class the
   budgets exist for. Deprecation or removal was the cleaner call; kept
   for back-compat without even a doc warning.
