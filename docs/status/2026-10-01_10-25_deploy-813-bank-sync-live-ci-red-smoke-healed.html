gTop question

QLC root: which space-recovery lever do you want pulled first?

The root sits at ~4.7 GiB unallocated — below the 5 GiB GC floor — so every GC and prune is blocked by design, the emergency reserve can't be re-created (it needs 10 GiB), and nix-build-cleanup aborts on most runs. The runbook order is: free extents → wait for quiet IO → one bounded balance → re-provision the reserve. "Free extents" today means either waiting for btrbk snapshot expiry, owner deletions (the Sep-12 recovery pattern), or accepting a bounded balance once the storm passes.

The question: do you want to reclaim space now (and if so, which lever — deletions you name, or the bounded balance at the next quiet window), or is riding at the floor until the next natural prune acceptable?
